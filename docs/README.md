# Usability test – MET-A-027

## Purpose

We conducted a usability test with Metro Service employees to see how
our system works for the people who would actually be using it.

The main purpose was to test whether stewards could quickly find the
relevant stations and tasks during a fallback scenario. We also wanted
to test the rest of the workflow, how information is communicated
between stewards and the control room, and whether the system fits
into their existing way of working.

## Participants

- 3 stewards
- 1 operator

The participants were actual Metro Service employees.

## Timing

We intended to time the individual tasks during the test.
However, the stewards often responded correctly or performed
the requested action almost immediately. In practice, we had
difficulty starting and stopping the timer between tasks,
especially when several actions were performed in succession.

We have included the timings we managed to record, but we do
not consider them reliable enough to calculate an overall
average or error rate.

The test therefore ended up providing more qualitative data
than quantitative data.

## Data handling

The participants are referred to as S1, S2, S3 and O1 throughout
the documentation.

The original recordings and full interview transcripts are
stored separately and are not included in this repository.

The documentation contains anonymised observations and
paraphrased feedback from the interviews.
