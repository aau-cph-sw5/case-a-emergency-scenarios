# Sprint 1 integration

**Groups 7, 9, 10 - Case A**

> **Retrospective note:** This document was filled out after Sprint 1.
> A formal cross-group integration process had not yet been established during
> the sprint.

## What happened

There was no formal integration meeting during Sprint 1.

Most of the sprint was spent getting familiar with Case A, understanding the
Metro domain and figuring out how Groups 7, 9 and 10 should divide and
coordinate the work.

The main shared work was around MET-A-001:

- The domain modelling was split into M1/M2 and M3/M4.
- Group 7 started the M1/M2 model.
- The models were intended to be reviewed together to create a shared
  understanding of the domain.
- This shared understanding was then used as input for MET-A-003 and the
  scenario state contract.

## Interfaces / contracts

Work on the shared scenario state contract (MET-A-003) was started based on
the domain understanding from MET-A-001.

At this point, the contract and integration process were still being worked
out.

## Dependencies found

- MET-A-001 required the M1/M2 and M3/M4 work to be brought together and
  reviewed.
- MET-A-003 depended on the shared domain understanding from MET-A-001.
- Work across the three groups needed clearer ownership and dependency
  tracking.

## Blockers

No formal shared blocker list was maintained during Sprint 1.

## Going into Sprint 2

For Sprint 2 we want to make integration more explicit by:

- having a shared integration meeting;
- making cross-group dependencies visible;
- keeping shared contracts/interfaces documented;
- checking what each group has changed that affects the others;
- identifying blockers earlier.
